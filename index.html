Keep Them Out!

The crooks are trying to sneak back in from across the border.
Tap them before they reach Bangladesh!

On iPhone: tap Share → Add to Home Screen to install.

Characters are placeholders. Satire / fan game.

How to play

  • Tap / click intruders to catch them and send them back.
  • If anyone crosses the border into Bangladesh — game over (unless you have a 🛡️ shield).
  • Catch quickly in a row for a combo: every 5 catches raises the multiplier (up to x5). Missed taps break the combo.
  • Catch someone right at the border: Close call +25.
  • Clear a wave: +50 × wave. 90%+ accuracy in a wave: +25 × wave.
  • Catches fill the ⚡ Sweep meter. When full, press it (or Space) to catch everyone on screen (half points, bosses take 4 damage).
  • A Boss arrives every 5th wave and keeps calling in cadres. Bosses always drop a power-up.
